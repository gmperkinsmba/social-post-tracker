# Product context

- An experiment links one audience, source brief, hypothesis, hook variants, and draft.
- A draft is editable text. Review evidence belongs to its exact text and source revision.
- Claims are reviewed by a person as source-backed or inference; the app does not verify truth.
- An outcome records platform, age, cohort, denominator, numerator counts, and evidence type.
- Results are comparable only when platform, denominator, and observation age match.
- Synthetic fixture outcomes are visibly labeled and never treated as measured business results.
- The product has no publishing or external analytics connection.
- Runtime state lives outside the source tree with owner-only filesystem permissions.
